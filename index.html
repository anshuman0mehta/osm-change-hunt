It is 02:00 at the constellation ops centre. Your SAR satellite crosses a 640 × 640 km sector in a few seconds. Something has changed down there, but you can only image 6 tiles of 64.

Your only clue is OpenStreetMap: millions of volunteers edit the world map within days of a change. Compare the map before (T₁) and after (T₂) and find the real changes.

Your rival is AUTO-PRIOR, the algorithm from our paper. It scores every edit by layer weight and fires at the six highest scores. Beat it.
