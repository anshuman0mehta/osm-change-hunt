How the OSM prior chooses

Each cell scores s(c) = Σ wℓ |Δℓ(c)|: every feature added or deleted between T₁ and T₂, weighted by layer. A modified feature counts as a delete plus a re-add. The prior tasks its 6 highest-scoring cells. In the paper it flags cells above the 90th percentile of non-zero scores.
